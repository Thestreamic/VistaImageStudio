How to use the studio

Open a photo. Leave the rest on this PC.

Tick a step when you have tried it. The films below are the real screens: the button you see is the button you press.

Open a picture with Ctrl+O, drop it on the window, or click a thumbnail in Media. iPhone HEIC is fine. Media keeps the photos on this computer even when you close the project.

Every other tool

These do not have their own film. The path is the label you already see in the app.

Natural Color

AI tab. A quieter grade. Skin and sun stay closer to the original.

Auto Color Correct

AI tab. Local white balance and levels when a photo looks cast.

Instagram 4:5

AI tab. Largest centred 4:5 crop. Confirm it in Transform.

Low Light

AI tab. Opens shadows on a dark photo.

Dehaze

AI tab. Cuts haze so distant detail comes back.

Clarity and Vibrance

AI tab. Clarity lifts local contrast. Vibrance lifts muted colour and leaves skin quieter.

Upscale 2×

AI tab. Enlarges the photo and resizes the document.

Denoise

Set Strength, then Apply Denoise. Use it on grain, not as a first step on a clean photo.

Face Enhance

Smoothing and Clarity sliders, then Apply Face Enhance.

Looks

Filters tab. Original, black and white, Warm, Cool. Intensity 0–100 blends back toward the original.

Adjust

Exposure, brightness, contrast, colour, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, sharpness. Curves are RGB or one channel. Reset clears them.

Crop

Press C. Free, 1:1 Post, 4:5 Portrait, 9:16 Story, 16:9 Thumb, or a custom ratio. Apply keeps it editable. Bake writes the pixels.

Move, select, pan

V moves the layer. M drags a box. W is the wand. H pans when you are zoomed in. The scroll wheel zooms.

Safe zones

Story, Reel, and TikTok chrome, so a title stays out of the buttons.

Text

Bold Headline, Elegant Subtitle, Pull Quote, Highlight Caption, Story Sticker, Minimal Caption. Then font, size, colour, highlight, outline.

Logo

Transform. A PNG or JPEG, or a text handle. Corners or centre, plus opacity and scale. Brand kit stores your colours and default font.

Layers

Show, lock, opacity, duplicate, reorder, delete. Blend modes include multiply, screen, overlay, and soft light.

Recipes and batch

Tools → Recipes saves the crop, look, and logo on this PC. Batch runs a recipe on a folder and skips a photo that fails.

Save, Export, Print, Make video

Keep the project, then publish the pixels

A .lumen file is the editable project. Export writes a new picture. Nothing in either step uploads the photo.

Ctrl+O open Ctrl+S save Ctrl+E export Ctrl+P print Ctrl+Z undo Ctrl+Shift+Z redo V move C crop M select W wand H hand

Privacy Centre, under Help, is the on-device check: no account, no cloud upload, no telemetry. Compare always shows the imported original, not a copy stored somewhere else.
