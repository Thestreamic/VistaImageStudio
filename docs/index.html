Built for creators who keep pixels local

Optimize Image, Magic Eraser, Portrait Bokeh, crop, looks, text, collages, export pack — all on-device.

✦

Optimize Image

One-click grade at the top of AI and Filters: brightness, colour, vibrance, clarity, sharpness. Natural Color sits next to it for a true-to-life look.

✂

Remove Background

On-device MODNet matting cuts the subject free on a transparent checkerboard. No cloud upload, no silent flood-fill fallback.

⌫

Magic Eraser

Box-select (M) or Magic Wand (W), then fill the hole with LaMa inpainting. Progress overlay; first run loads a local ~208MB model.

◎

Portrait Bokeh

Depth-based disc bokeh: MiDaS depth + MODNet subject — sharp person, soft falloff, glowing highlight orbs.

▣

Live crop + Instagram 4:5

1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9, custom — move and resize handles. One-click Instagram 4:5 from AI; Story safe-zone overlay.

✨

Looks

Original, B&W, Warm, Cool with 0–100 intensity. Optimize Image is pinned at the top of Filters.

◐

Full adjust stack

Exposure, brightness, contrast, sat, temp, tint, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, vibrance, sharpness + RGB curves.

▤

Layers, text & collages

Blend modes, opacity, lock, templates (headline, quote, sticker…), brand kit, collage layouts. Move frames; double-click Media to fill boxes in order. File → Print (Ctrl+P).

💾

.lumen projects

Save / Open / Save As with dirty flag, autosave, recents, and a quit prompt so you don’t lose work.

📦

Smart social export

IG / Stories / YouTube / Pinterest / LinkedIn / X / Facebook pack, carousel split, handle stamp, EXIF-free canvas export.

◑

Compare

Hold Compare to peek at the original. Click for a before/after split and pick how many history steps back.

📷

Import anything common

PNG, JPEG, WebP, HEIC (iPhone), AVIF, BMP, GIF, TIFF — drag into the window, Open, or drop a folder into the media bin.

📖

Recipes & batch

Save crop + look + logo as a local recipe. Apply a recipe to a folder of photos; failures are skipped.

🛡

Privacy Centre

In-app audit: analytics off, CSP/paths, first-run walkthrough. Photos and models never leave the device.

🏷

Logo & handle

PNG/JPEG logo or text watermark as a live overlay — corner, opacity, and scale, included in history and recipes.

How to use (30 seconds)

  1. Open a photo (or drop it — HEIC from iPhone is fine).
  2. AI or Filters → Optimize Image.
  3. Optional: Portrait Bokeh, Remove Background, or Magic Eraser (Select M / Wand W first).
  4. Transform → crop 4:5 (or AI → Instagram 4:5, then confirm).
  5. Optional: Warm / Cool / B&W, Adjust sliders, text, or logo.
  6. Save as .lumen (Ctrl+S) → Export (Ctrl+E) Creator pack.
