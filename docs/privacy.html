Vista Image Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE] · Applies to Vista Image Studio, currently distributed as a public beta.

Short version: Your images never leave your device. Editing and AI processing run locally. We do not operate servers that receive, store, or view your photos.

1. What we do not collect

  • The images, photos, or files you open, edit, or export.
  • The content of any layer, adjustment, or AI operation you apply.
  • Your file system paths or folder structure.

All image processing — adjustments, curves, transforms, and every AI feature (background removal, denoise, upscale, face enhance, object removal, smart select, auto color) — executes locally on your device, in-process or in a local Web Worker. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

2. What limited data may be collected

Depending on the version and settings you use, the following limited, non-content data may apply:

DataPurposeSent where
App version checkNotify you of updatesGitHub Releases API (public, read-only)
Crash diagnostics (if enabled)Fix bugs during betaNot enabled by default

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or third-party trackers in the Software.

3. Optional AI model downloads

Some AI features can use optional, higher-quality on-device models. If you choose to enable a model-backed mode, the Software may download the model file itself (not your images) from a hosting location such as GitHub Releases or Hugging Face. This is a one-time file download for the model binary, distinct from image processing, which still happens entirely on your device afterward.

4. Data storage

Any application settings or preferences are stored locally on your device (e.g. in your OS's standard application data folder). We do not maintain user accounts or cloud-synced profiles during the beta.

5. Third-party services

The Software may make outbound network requests to check for updates against the public GitHub Releases API, and, if you opt into model-backed AI features, to download model files from their hosting provider. These requests do not include your images or personal content.

6. Children's privacy

The Software is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes during and after beta

This Privacy Policy reflects the beta release. The commercial release planned for after the beta period may introduce optional cloud features (e.g. cloud backup, account sync). Any such feature will be opt-in, clearly disclosed, and accompanied by an updated Privacy Policy before it is enabled by default for any user.

8. Your choices

Because the Software does not require an account or transmit your content, there is no server-side data of yours to request, export, or delete. Uninstalling the Software removes its locally stored settings.
